About Jain Chaat Bhandar

Located on Naval Kishore Road at Novelty Chauraha, Lalbagh, Jain Chaat Bhandar is one of Lucknow's best-known addresses for Jain chaat. The shop is loved for keeping its menu short, fresh and consistent — the reason evenings here draw a steady crowd of regulars and visitors alike.

What makes the chaat special is the Jain preparation: no onion and no garlic are used, and a squeeze of lemon brings out a light, clean flavour that is quite different from ordinary chaat. It is a small shop with a big reputation — simple, honest and delicious.
